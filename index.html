STICK LEGACY

MINE GOLD  ·  TRAIN YOUR ARMY  ·  TOPPLE THE ENEMY STATUE

A fan tribute to classic stick-war RTS gameplay · every animation drawn in code

HOW TO PLAY

Your statue is your life. Grow an economy with miners, train an army, and smash the enemy statue before they smash yours.

CONTROLS

  • 1–6 Train units (cards, bottom)
  • F / G All-out ATTACK / DEFEND orders
  • Click Select one of your units
  • Double-click or C Take direct control
  • AD Move controlled unit · Space attack / mine
  • Right-click Move / attack-move order for selected unit
  • ←→ or drag · Pan camera · H base · N front line
  • Esc Release control · P pause · M mute

GOLD

Miners walk to the gold mine, swing their picks, and haul loads back to your statue. More miners = faster army. Guard them — a dead miner is 70 gold wasted.
